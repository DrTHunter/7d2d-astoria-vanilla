# Traders

38 traders, at least one within a short walk of every town on the map. 24 came with Astoria;
14 were added and 3 of the originals were given a better building in the same spot.

Astoria's own traders sit a median 66 m outside the town edge, on the approach road, so the new
ones follow that: a clear, flat 60x60 patch 25-140 m beyond the edge, scored on distance to the
nearest road, how much cut and fill it needs, and whether it faces the next town along. Every
site chosen is on or beside a road and all but two need under a metre of levelling. An existing
town lot was the fallback and never won.

All 14 distinct trader buildings are in use, so no two neighbouring towns look alike. Three of
them have deep basements - MPLogue's Wight bunker goes 31 blocks down - so those went to the
sites with the most ground beneath them; the shallowest trader now stands on 5 m of rock.

Teleport with `tp <x> <y> <z>` from the console (F1), or turn on the **Traders** layer on the
[interactive map](https://drthunter.github.io/astoria-8k-poi-map/).
